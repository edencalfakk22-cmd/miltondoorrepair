Where we work in Milton

From Old Milton, Bronte Meadows, Timberlea, Dorset Park, Beaty, Clarke, Coates, Dempsey, Harrison, Scott, Willmott and Derry Green Business Park, we cover every Milton neighbourhood — plus the surrounding Halton communities.

Derry Green and the 401 distribution corridor. Derry Green Business Park south of the 401 is Milton's largest employment area at over 2,000 acres, purpose-zoned for logistics, warehousing and distribution, and the buildings going up there are full of doors that never rest. Personnel doors beside every dock, vestibule entrances into the office block, hollow metal doors between the warehouse and the packing area — on a two-shift operation those cycle hundreds of times a day and wear out on a schedule, not by accident. We treat them as maintenance items across the James Snow Parkway and Steeles corridor, because a dock man-door that will not latch is a security and heat-loss problem in the same breath.

Warehouse pressure, forklift traffic and doors that get abused. Distribution buildings are hard on doors in ways an office never is. Pallet jacks and forklifts clip frames, carts get shoved through half-open leaves, and big rooftop units running against open dock doors put the whole building under pressure, so an interior door either slams or refuses to close the last inch. We straighten and reinforce hollow metal doors and frames, fit the right closer for the actual pressure at that opening rather than the one that came in the box, and add protection plates where the damage keeps repeating.
