About Escarpment Door & Hardware

Escarpment Door and Hardware started the way most door companies do: with the service calls nobody else wanted. A Main Street shop that could not lock up at closing. A dock man-door in a Steeles Avenue warehouse that had been wedged with a pallet for so long that nobody remembered it was a fire door. A two-year-old house in Ford where every interior door had gone out of square in the same winter.

We are a Milton door repair outfit, not a general contractor with a van. We work on aluminum storefronts, hollow metal doors and frames, hinges, pivots, closers, thresholds, locks and exit hardware — commercial and residential — across Milton, Halton Region and the wider Greater Toronto Area. We fix what actually caused the problem, which on a Milton door is usually the frame, the pivot or the season rather than the lock everyone blames.

We are fully insured and WSIB covered, we guarantee our workmanship in writing, and we quote a fixed scope after looking at the opening. No estimate over the phone that changes once we arrive, and no upsell to a new door when the one you have has years left in it.

Why Milton property owners trust us

  • Warehouse, storefront and office doors are our daily work, not a sideline
  • We repair the frame, pivot and threshold — not just the lock everyone blames
  • Fire doors returned to self-closing and latching, as the Ontario Fire Code requires
  • Closers set for real Milton winter operation, so doors still latch below minus fifteen
  • Insured, WSIB covered, with a written workmanship guarantee
  • Same-day and after-hours response when a door will not lock or latch

Insured & WSIB-covered

We carry full liability insurance and WSIB coverage, we keep exit doors and their hardware operating the way they are meant to, and we guarantee our workmanship in writing — serving property owners across Milton and the Greater Toronto Area.
