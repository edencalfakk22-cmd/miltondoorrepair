FAQs

Commercial Locks & Exit Devices — your questions

Our storefront will not lock at closing time — what usually causes it?

Most often the door has dropped on a worn pivot, or the frame has shifted, so the deadlatch is missing the strike. Cold weather makes it worse because everything contracts. We re-align the door, correct the strike and repair or replace the lock so it secures properly again rather than just swapping the cylinder.

Can you service panic bars and exit devices in a Milton warehouse?

Yes — repair, adjustment and replacement of panic and exit hardware on commercial doors, including a walk-through of every exit in the building. A door on an exit route has to open from the inside in one motion, so a sticking or seized device is a life-safety issue rather than routine maintenance.

Do you install electric strikes and buzzer entry on commercial doors?

Yes, on aluminum, hollow metal and glass entrance doors, including correcting the door and frame so the strike lines up reliably in the cold as well as in summer. We coordinate with your access-control or alarm provider where one is already in place.
