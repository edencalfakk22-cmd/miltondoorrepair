We repair and replace deadlatches, mortise locks, cylinders, electric strikes, panic bars and exit devices on aluminum, hollow metal and glass entrance doors — from a single unit on Main Street to a full walk-through of every exit door in a distribution building before an inspection.

FAQs

Commercial Locks & Exit Devices — your questions

Can exit hardware be serviced without shutting the dock down?

Usually. Exit device work is mostly done at the leaf, so we book it around the shift and, on a run of doors, work through them one at a time so the building never loses more than one exit at once. Anything that would leave an exit unavailable is scheduled outside operating hours.

Our locks are all on different keys across three units. Can that be simplified?

Yes, provided the cylinders are a current commercial format. They can be changed over to one common key, or into a straightforward master arrangement where you hold one key and each unit keeps its own. Where a cylinder is worn out or the format has no keying parts left, that one gets replaced and brought into the same system.
