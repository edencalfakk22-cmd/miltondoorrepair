It sticks and it will not latch are the two calls we take most in Milton, and they are usually the same problem seen in two different seasons. Humid summers swell wood doors and older frames until they bind; a January cold snap contracts everything, the closer slows, and the deadlatch stops reaching the strike. In between, freeze-thaw keeps nudging the frame, so an opening that was fine last year is out by a few millimetres this year.

In the newer subdivisions there is a third cause on top of the weather: a house that is still settling. Doors in Ford, Cobban, Walker, Harrison and Coates commonly go out of square in their first couple of winters, and builder-grade hinges and strikes are the first things to let go. We diagnose the whole assembly — hinges, pivots, frame square, strike position, threshold height and closer settings — and correct the cause instead of moving the strike plate and hoping. On commercial doors we set the closer so it still latches when the fluid is cold and thick.

FAQs

Door Alignment & Latch Repair — your questions

My door sticks in the summer and rattles in the winter — what is going on?

That is classic Halton seasonal movement. Humid summer air swells the door and frame, then winter cold shrinks everything back and the gaps open up. The fix is to set the door for the swing it actually has year-round — correcting hinge position, frame square and strike — rather than trimming it for one season.

Our house is only a few years old and the doors already stick. Is that normal in Milton?

It is very common in the newer parts of town. A new home settles for its first couple of years, frames move a few millimetres, and the light-duty hinges and strikes builders fit are the first to complain. Re-shimming the hinge side, correcting the strike and re-hanging the leaf usually solves it without replacing the door.

The door closes but does not latch — is that just the lock?

Rarely. Nine times out of ten the door is sitting low or out of square so the latch arrives below or behind the strike, or a cold closer stalls the door short of it. We check the alignment first and only replace hardware when the hardware is genuinely the failure.
